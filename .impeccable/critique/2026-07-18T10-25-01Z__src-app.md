## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Fail. The football content is authentic, but the composition is visibly AI-shaped: large-radius cards, nested mini-cards, oversized metrics, wide shadows and repeated tiny uppercase labels resemble a generated SaaS dashboard.

**Deterministic scan:** 68 findings across `src/app`: 62 off-system font sizes, two undocumented shadow colors, three gray-on-color warnings and one side-tab warning. The gray-on-amber and side-tab findings are verified false positives. The font-size drift and broad bordered-surface shadows are genuine.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable user-visible overlay was created because the sub-agent browser backend exposed no tabs. Earlier authenticated inspection in the parent session confirmed the same card-heavy hierarchy on desktop and mobile, including blank KPI placeholder panels.

## Overall Impression

The product has a strong domain model and a coherent shell. Its largest opportunity is to let the current matchday, the next action and personal performance lead, while reducing generic container decoration and secondary analytical noise.

## What's Working

- Domain-first IA: Spieltag, Tippen and Profil form a clear primary path.
- The tip form already has strong numeric constraints, autosave, Joker safeguards and responsive structures.
- shadcn primitives, semantic theme tokens, local Inter and reduced-motion support provide a solid foundation.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Core tip flow is not screen-reader safe

- **Why it matters:** Home/away score inputs depend on visual position and autosave changes are silent.
- **Fix:** Group fixtures semantically, add team-specific accessible names, explicit Joker labels and an `aria-live` save status.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit`

### [P1] Season deletion executes immediately

- **Why it matters:** An administrator can trigger an irreversible action without clear confirmation or consequence text.
- **Fix:** Add a shadcn AlertDialog naming the season and require explicit confirmation.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Visual grammar contradicts the documented system

- **Why it matters:** Broad shadows, radii above 14px and 48px page headings make the app feel generic and reduce density.
- **Fix:** Flatten content surfaces, cap radii, reduce titles and remove repeating eyebrow/card scaffolding.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P2] Admin and Stats lack a primary story

- **Why it matters:** Eight peer Admin cards and simultaneous analytics create a high cognitive load.
- **Fix:** Lead Admin with the active matchday and operational destinations; lead Stats with personal performance and simplify secondary panels.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Registration completion is ambiguous and cramped on mobile

- **Why it matters:** The success query is ignored and two form fields remain in two columns on narrow screens.
- **Fix:** Show a success alert on login and stack the name fields until the small breakpoint.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

## Closed Priority Findings

- Tip score inputs and Joker controls now have fixture-specific accessible names; autosave uses `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"`.
- Season deletion uses a shadcn AlertDialog naming the irreversible consequence.
- Repeated Admin season, deadline and status controls have explicit programmatic labels.
- Club selection exposes a stable `Lieblingsclub` accessible name even after a club is chosen.
- Framer Motion entry and status animations honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; CSS transitions have the global reduced-motion fallback.
- Secondary status badges use the required dark semantic foreground.
- Coarse-pointer buttons, Sidebar targets, inputs and select triggers receive a 44 px minimum target height.
- The Stats Joker panel and player KPIs were distilled; charts use 12 px labels and tabular numeric features.
